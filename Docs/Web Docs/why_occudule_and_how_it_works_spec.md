# Core Page Specification: How It Works & Why Occudule

This document outlines the structured marketing copy and component requirements for the **How It Works** and **Why Occudule** sections, fully synchronized with the design tokens and layout rules defined in the core project specification.

---

## 1. How It Works

### Step 1: Connect Your Provider
* **Component Design:** Two-column split layout or card selectors featuring clear Microsoft and Google branding vectors.
* **Content Nodes:**
  * **Microsoft Accounts (Outlook, Live, MSN, Hotmail):** Connect your account securely just once. Occudule will automatically scan and analyze incoming messages.
  * **Gmail Accounts:** Simply add your unique Occudule forwarding address to your contacts and forward your child-related emails with a single tap.

### Step 2: Intelligent Processing & Analysis
* **Component Design:** Sequence layout block or 3-part clean vertical rhythm.
* **Content Nodes:**
  * **Smart Scoring System:** Occudule filters through the noise, instantly identifying emails from schools, teams, or academies based on a proprietary smart scoring system.
  * **Dual-Categorization:** Messages are stripped of clutter, summarized, and cleanly organized into two intuitive streams: **Events** (time-sensitive schedules) and **Info** (reference material).
  * **Actionable Data Extraction:** Key details are analyzed and automatically grouped to generate ready-to-use to-do lists, calendar events, reference info cards, and smart email replies.

### Step 3: Automated Execution
* **Component Design:** Broad full-width terminal or application preview container showcasing simulated execution.
* **Content Nodes:**
  * **Proactive Reminders:** Receive timely, automated alerts for upcoming events and critical deadlines before they sneak up on you.
  * **A Centralized Family Hub:** Your entire household's logistics live in one secure, unified space. Say goodbye to scattered apps, manual calendar entries, and messy copy-pasting.

## 2. Why Occudule

### 1. Everything in One Centralized Space
* **Content:** Forget the old routine of opening an email, manually creating a calendar event, copying notes into a separate app, typing out reminders, and holding stressful family meetings to sync schedules. Occudule replaces the chaos, handling your entire workflow in one beautiful, cohesive experience.

### 2. Built with a Privacy-First Mindset
* **Content:** We respect your personal data. To minimize data collection, Occudule extracts only the essential logistics and action items rather than storing your entire email history. Backed by strict internal data management policies, we ensure full compliance with modern data regulations to keep your information safe.

### 3. Made by Parents, for Parents
* **Content:** We lived the exact same daily logistical chaos you are experiencing. Grounded in our core philosophy—*"Made by parents, for parents"*—we engineered Occudule with deep empathy and a singular goal: to eliminate the mental burden of family logistics.

### 4. Optimized Specially for School & Extracurriculars
* **Content:** Unlike generic AI tools, Occudule's specialized workflows are purpose-built to navigate the unique nuances of school announcements, permission slips, sports leagues, and after-school programs.
