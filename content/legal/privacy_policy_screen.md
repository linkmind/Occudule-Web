# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 22, 2026**

## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how **Outvblue Technology Inc.** ("Outvblue," "we," "us," or "our"), a corporation **registered in Ontario, Canada**, collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our **Occudule** mobile application and related services (the "service").

Occudule is designed for parents and guardians managing family and school-related information. We take privacy seriously, including information about children that you choose to add to your account.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the service. Where local law requires a separate legal basis or consent (for example, optional crash diagnostics), we will rely on consent or another basis as described below.

### Connected email accounts (Gmail and Outlook)

**Forwarding messages to Occudule**

- We provide you with a **personal inbound email address** on our domain so you can **forward** school-related messages from your mailbox (whether you use Gmail, Outlook, or another client). The address is tied to your account and is designed so only you can use it as intended.
- When you forward an email, the **content** of that message is received by a **provider we use to receive inbound email** and is processed through our **existing email filtering and extraction pipeline**. We may **verify** that the message appears to come from the **sync email address** you configured in the app before we treat it as yours.
- **Adding calendar events** from mail that arrived via this **forward** path typically requires your **confirmation in the app** (for example, on an Event Confirmation screen) before a new event is saved — as described in the product.

**Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365**

- If you connect Outlook, we use the permissions you grant to access messages as needed to provide the app’s features (for example, **subject lines, senders, and message content**) to extract school-related events and to-dos, consistent with your connection and our in-app descriptions.

**Google Gmail**

- If you connect Gmail, we use the **Google permissions you grant** for the features shown when you connect (for example, mail or calendar sync, if enabled). Those features are separate from **forwarding**: you may **forward** specific messages to your Occudule inbound address so we can process them through the same pipeline as other mail you send us.

| Data Category | What We Collect (email-related summary) |
|---|---|
| **Forwarded email** | The **content** of messages you forward to your Occudule address, plus headers needed to process them and to verify they match your configured sync email where we apply that check |
| **Connected account (Gmail / Outlook)** | Per the permissions you authorize at connection time, which may include message content for **sync** features as described in the app |

We also collect **device type**, **OS and app version**, **language**, **time zone**, and similar technical data needed to run and secure the service; **push notification tokens** if you enable notifications; and, if you opt in under Settings, **crash and stability diagnostics** processed by **Google Firebase Crashlytics** (see the in-app "Crash reporting" screen for details and how to withdraw consent).

### In-app live chat (Zoho SalesIQ)

When you choose **Live chat** from **Contact Us** in the app, the chat experience is delivered through **Zoho SalesIQ** (Mobilisten), operated by **Zoho Corporation** and its affiliates as a service provider to Outvblue. **Zoho** may process the content of your chat messages, technical identifiers associated with your device or session, IP address, and other data described in **[Zoho’s Privacy Policy](https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html)** to operate chat, route conversations to our support team, and help prevent abuse. Processing for that feature occurs on Zoho’s systems according to Zoho’s policies. **Please avoid sharing payment card numbers, account passwords, or unnecessary sensitive personal information** in chat; you may use **Send feedback** (email) when a live conversation is not needed.

If you paste or describe information about children in chat, you do so at your own discretion; we ask that you share only what is needed for your support request.

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **AI extraction:** To summarize school-related emails and help create calendar events (including from mail you **forward** into Occudule or from **connected** mailboxes, as described in the app). Where the app requires it, we ask you to **confirm** before a new event is added. These AI features are provided through **OpenAI’s API** (for example, classification, extraction, summarization, translation, and optional draft replies). We send only the email content and related fields needed for that in-product processing. We do **not** use your data—or allow it to be used—to create, train, or improve foundational or generalized AI/ML models. Under OpenAI’s API terms, API inputs and outputs are not used by OpenAI to train or improve their models by default, and we do not opt in to any OpenAI data-sharing or training programs.
- **Event management:** To detect scheduling conflicts between your children's activities.
- **Notifications & alerts:** To send reminders and notifications about upcoming school deadlines and events.
- **Rule matching:** To compare incoming mail (including forwarded mail and synced mail, depending on the feature) against domains and allowlists **you** entered in child profile settings.
- **Subscriptions:** To validate entitlements and provide support using RevenueCat and store APIs, as described above.
- **Customer support:** To respond when you contact us, including through optional **live chat** operated by **Zoho SalesIQ** as described above.

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your email content for third-party advertising profiling.

### Google Workspace APIs and Limited Use

If you connect a Google account, Occudule may use Google Workspace APIs (such as Contacts and Calendar) only to provide the features you enable in the app (for example, creating a forwarding contact and calendar free/busy or event sync). **The use of raw or derived user data received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.**

We do **not** transfer Google Workspace API user data (raw, aggregated, or derived) to OpenAI or any other third-party AI/ML service for model training or secondary purposes. Calendar and Contacts data obtained via Google APIs is used only to operate Occudule’s scheduling and contact features within our application and Google Calendar sync. We do **not** use Google user data to create, train, or improve foundational or generalized AI/ML models.
