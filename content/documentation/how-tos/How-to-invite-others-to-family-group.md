---
title: How to Invite Others to Your Occudule Family Group
description: Invite family members to your Occudule Family Group on Premium or Diamond and help them accept from Gmail or Microsoft email to co-manage school schedules.
order: 4
---

Family Group lets you invite another parent, guardian, or caregiver to help manage your children's school schedules in Occudule. This guide covers sending an invitation as the family group owner and accepting an invitation as the person you invited.

---

## Before you begin

**Family group owners**

* Family Group is available on **Premium** or **Diamond** plans. See [how to upgrade your Occudule account](/documentation/how-tos/upgrade-free-to-premium-diamond) if you are still on the free plan.
* You can invite family members who use **Gmail** or a **Microsoft email account** (Outlook, Hotmail, Live, or MSN).

**Invited family members**

* Use the same email address that received the invitation.
* Check your inbox and junk or spam folder for a message from Occudule.
* The **Join family** link in the email expires after **7 days**.

---

## Step 1: Open Family Profile

As the family group owner:

1. From the home screen, tap **Profile**.
2. Select **Family Profile**.
3. Scroll to the **Family Group** section at the bottom of the screen.

---

## Step 2: Send a family group invitation

1. Tap **Invite family member**.
2. In the pop-up window, enter the family member's email address.
   * The address must be a **Gmail** or **Microsoft** account (Outlook, Hotmail, Live, or MSN).
3. *(Optional)* Add a relationship label.
4. Tap **Send invitation**.

When the invitation sends successfully, you will see a confirmation message. Tap **OK**.

After sending the invitation, follow up with your family member and remind them to look for the Occudule email in their inbox or junk mail.

---

## Step 3: Open the invitation email

As the invited family member:

1. Open the invitation email from Occudule.
2. Tap **Join family**.
3. Continue with the steps below based on whether you already have an Occudule account.

---

## Step 4: Join as a new Occudule user

Choose this path if you do **not** already have an Occudule account.

1. On the invitation screen, tap **Continue**.
2. Create a password for your Occudule account.
   * Your login email is the address that received the invitation.
3. On the welcome screen, choose one option:
   * **Download on the App Store** — if you do not have the Occudule app yet.
   * **Open Occudule** — if the app is already installed.
4. Sign in to the app.

You are now part of the family group.

---

## Step 5: Join with an existing account (no child profiles)

Choose this path if you already have an Occudule account but have **not** added any child profiles yet.

### Social sign-in (Google, Microsoft, or Apple)

1. On the **You're invited** screen, confirm your email address and tap **Continue**.
2. Tap **Open Occudule** on the **Sign in with the app** screen.
3. On the **Join Family** screen, tap **Sign in to accept**.
4. Sign in with the same social provider you used when you created your Occudule account.
5. Return to the **Join Family** screen and tap **Accept invitation**.

### Email and password sign-in

1. On the **You're invited** screen, tap **Continue**.
2. On the **Sign in to join** screen, enter your Occudule password.
3. Tap **Join family**.
4. On the welcome screen, download or open the Occudule app and sign in.

You are now part of the family group.

---

## Step 6: Join with an existing account (with child profiles)

Choose this path if you already have an Occudule account **and** one or more child profiles.

After you tap **Continue** on the **You're invited** screen, you will see **How would you like to join** with two options:

### Option 1: Join on the group owner's plan

Choose this option if the children on your account are the **same** children already on the group owner's account.

* All child profiles on your account will be removed.
* You will join the family group using the group owner's child profiles.
* Email forwarding (Gmail) or inbox syncing (Microsoft) on your account will be turned off.
* Your calendar connection will remain linked.

### Option 2: Bring child profiles into the family group

Choose this option if you want to keep **different** children on the shared family account.

* You can move up to **3 child profiles** into the family group.
* Any additional child profiles (the 4th and above) will be removed after you join.

**Which option should I choose?**

* **Same children on both accounts?** Choose **Join on the group owner's plan**.
* **Different children you want to keep?** Choose **Bring child profiles into the family group**.

### Finish joining

**Social sign-in users**

1. Tap **Open Occudule**, then **Sign in to accept**.
2. Sign in with your usual social provider.
3. Select your join option and confirm the summary message.
4. Tap **Accept invitation**.

**Email and password users**

1. Select your join option and tap **OK** on the confirmation message.
2. On the **Sign in to join** screen, enter your password.
3. Tap **Join family**.
4. Download or open the Occudule app and sign in.

You are now part of the family group.

---
