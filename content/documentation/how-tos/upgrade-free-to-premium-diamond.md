Welcome to the exciting journey of upgrading your Occudule account! We're here to guide you smoothly through each step so you can enjoy additional features and benefits in no time.

## Step 1: Access Your Profile

- Tap the **Profile** icon at the top right corner of the home page.
- Select **Subscription** from the menu.

## Step 2: Explore the Plan Options

- Tap on **View & Change Plans** to explore the variety of options we offer.
- Choose a plan that fits your needs:
  - Premium Monthly
  - Premium Yearly
  - Diamond Monthly
  - Diamond Yearly

Each plan comes with its own set of features, so take your time to review and find what best suits your needs.

## Step 3: Secure Your Plan

- Tap **Continue** to proceed with the payment.
- Rest assured, your payment is processed securely through Apple App's trusted services. We never store your banking information!



## Step 4: Confirm Your Purchase

You'll see a confirmation message saying, “Your purchase was successful.” Simply click **OK** to move on.

## Step 5: Sync Your Email for Seamless Integration

With your upgraded plan, it's time to sync your email for seamless event tracking:

- You'll notice a **Success** message reminding you to reconnect your email. Tap **Open Profile**.
- On the **User Profile** page, find **Email Account (for syncing)** under **Syncing | Integration**.
- Follow the easy prompts to grant Occudule access to your calendar.
- A final message will confirm “Your email account is linked for syncing.”

Congratulations! You've now upgraded your account.
