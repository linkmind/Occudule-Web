---
title: How to Connect Gmail or Microsoft Email After Sign in with Apple
description: Signed in to Occudule with Apple using iCloud or Hide My Email? Enter a Gmail or Microsoft address in Email Account (for syncing) so school mail can connect.
order: 5
---

Sign in with Apple creates your Occudule account. It does **not** connect school email. Occudule syncs school messages from a **Gmail** or **Microsoft** mailbox (Outlook, Hotmail, Live, or MSN).

If **Email Account (for syncing)** is empty after Apple sign-in, the app is working as designed. Your Apple login (including iCloud or Hide My Email) cannot be used for syncing. Enter a supported mailbox, then connect it.

You will see this when:

* You chose **Hide My Email** (`@privaterelay.appleid.com`).
* You signed in with **iCloud** or another address that is not Gmail or Microsoft.
* A reminder under the box says your Apple sign-in address cannot be used for syncing.

If Apple already shared a Gmail or Microsoft address, that address is filled in for you. Skip to [Step 4](#step-4-connect-the-mailbox).

New to Occudule? Start with [how to create and set up your free Occudule account](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-set-up-your-free-occudule-account).

---

## Step 1: Open User Profile

1. From the home screen, tap **Profile**.
2. Select **User Profile**.

---

## Step 2: Find Email Account (for syncing)

On the **User Profile** screen, scroll down to the **Syncing | Integration** section.

* The **account email** at the top is the address you used with Apple. It is read-only.
* **Email Account (for syncing)** is the mailbox Occudule uses for school mail. If it is empty, a reminder appears under the box: *"Enter a Gmail or Microsoft email here to sync school mail. Your Apple sign-in address cannot be used for syncing."*

---

## Step 3: Enter a Gmail or Microsoft email

1. Tap **Email Account (for syncing)**.
2. Type a **Gmail** or **Microsoft** address (Outlook, Hotmail, Live, or MSN).
3. Do **not** enter iCloud, Hide My Email, or another unsupported address. Those stay in your Apple login field only.

---

## Step 4: Connect the mailbox

When the address is supported, email syncing shows **Not connected yet**.

1. Tap **"here"** (highlighted in green in *"Tap here to connect and start syncing"*).
2. A confirmation prompt will ask you to allow Google or Microsoft to share information with Occudule. Tap **Continue**.
3. Sign in with the **same** Gmail or Microsoft account you typed in the box.
4. When linking succeeds, you should see *"Your email account is linked for syncing."* Tap **OK**.

Apple sign-in is not used in this step. You are connecting Gmail or Microsoft for mail sync.

---

## Step 5: Finish Gmail or Microsoft setup

* **Microsoft (Outlook, Live, MSN, or Hotmail):** Setup is complete. Occudule can process incoming school mail that matches the schools and institutions on your Family Profile.
* **Gmail:** Forward school emails to your Occudule address. On User Profile, use **Add to Contacts** in the **Email Forwarding** section so **Occudule Forward** is saved in Google Contacts. Details are in [how to create and set up your free Occudule account](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-set-up-your-free-occudule-account).

If a later connection drops, follow [how to reconnect your email account in Occudule](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-reconnect-email-account). To see what Occudule does with school mail after you connect, review the [Occudule features outline](/documentation/features/occudule-features-outline).
