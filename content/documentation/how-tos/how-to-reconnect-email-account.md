---
title: How to Reconnect Your Email Account in Occudule
description: Reconnect Gmail or Microsoft email in Occudule after a session expires, a plan upgrade, or a lost connection so school email and calendar sync resume.
order: 3
---

This step-by-step guide walks you through reconnecting the email account Occudule uses for school-message syncing. You may need to do this when:

* **Your session expired.** Occudule signed you out of your email provider for security.
* **You upgraded your plan.** A [Premium or Diamond upgrade](/documentation/how-tos/upgrade-free-to-premium-diamond) can require extra permissions, such as calendar sync.
* **The connection was reset.** Occudule can no longer reach the Gmail, Outlook, Live, MSN, or Hotmail account you linked for syncing.

---

## Step 1: Open User Profile

1. From the home screen, tap **Profile** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **User Profile**.

---

## Step 2: Find Syncing and Integration

On the **User Profile** screen, scroll down to the **Syncing | Integration** section.

---

## Step 3: Start Reconnection

Tap the box that shows your linked email address (labeled **Email Account (for syncing)**).

---

## Step 4: Authorize Access

A confirmation prompt will ask you to allow your email provider to share information with Occudule.

* **Gmail:** You may see *"Occudule wants to use accounts.google.com to Sign in"*. Tap **Continue**.
* **Microsoft (Outlook, Live, MSN, or Hotmail):** Follow the Microsoft sign-in prompt, then tap **Continue**.

---

## Step 5: Complete Sign-In

1. Select the same email address already linked to your Occudule account.
2. Follow the on-screen prompts to grant permission.

When linking succeeds, you should see *"Your email account is linked for syncing."* Tap **OK**. Email syncing—and calendar sync, if your plan includes it—resumes automatically.

If you have not connected an email account yet, start with [how to create and set up your free Occudule account](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-set-up-your-free-occudule-account). To see what calendar sync and related features include, review the [Occudule features outline](/documentation/features/occudule-features-outline).
