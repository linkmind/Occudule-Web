## Header

The header sits at the top of every main screen. It has two menus: the **Child picker** on the left and **Profile** on the right.

### Child picker menu

The child picker is in the **top-left** corner. It lists every child on your account, plus **All**.

**All** is selected by default. In that view, Occudule shows events, info, and to-dos for every child. Color-coded tags on cards show which child each item belongs to. If you uploaded a photo for a child, that photo appears next to the name.

Tap the picker, then tap a child's name to see only that child's events, info, and to-dos.

How many children you can add depends on your plan (one on Free, more on Premium and Diamond). See [how to upgrade your Occudule account](/documentation/how-tos/upgrade-free-to-premium-diamond).

### Profile menu

**Profile** is in the **top-right** corner. Tap it to open:

#### User Profile

**User Profile** is your account and integrations screen.

- Review your name. Your **account email** (the address you signed in with) is read-only.
- Under **Syncing | Integration**, check **Email Account (for syncing)**. That Gmail or Microsoft mailbox is what Occudule uses for syncing school mail. It is separate from Sign in with Apple.
- Connect or reconnect email from this screen. If you signed in with Apple using iCloud or Hide My Email, the sync box stays empty until you enter a Gmail or Microsoft address. See [how to connect email after Sign in with Apple](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-connect-email-after-sign-in-with-apple) and [how to reconnect your email account](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-reconnect-email-account).
- Premium and Diamond users can also connect Google or Outlook Calendar here for conflict checks and calendar sync.

Family group **members** do not manage email sync. They can still connect a calendar. The family owner processes school email for the household.

#### Family Profile

**Family Profile** is where you manage children and the household.

- Add or edit each child's name, grade, grade start date, school, and school email domain. Occudule uses those names and domains to recognize school mail.
- Add other education institutions (clubs, teams, or programs) per child, with their own domains and keywords.
- Scroll to **Family Group** to invite another parent, guardian, or caregiver. Family Group is available on **Premium** or **Diamond**. Follow [how to invite others to your Occudule family group](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-invite-others-to-family-group).

You land on Family Profile during setup. You can return anytime from the Profile menu.

#### Subscription

**Subscription** shows your current plan (Free, Premium, or Diamond) and lets you change it.

- Tap **View & Change Plans** to compare monthly and yearly Premium or Diamond.
- Purchases go through the **App Store**. Occudule does not store your card number.

After an upgrade, reconnect email and calendar if the app asks you to. The full flow is in [how to upgrade your Occudule account](/documentation/how-tos/upgrade-free-to-premium-diamond). Plan limits (children, email processing, conflict detection, calendar sync) are summarized in the [Occudule features outline](/documentation/features/occudule-features-outline).

#### Billing Help

**Billing Help** covers App Store subscription settings, receipts, and restore. In-app subscriptions are billed by Apple, so invoices and cancellations live in your Apple ID subscription settings, not inside Occudule.

If a purchase does not show in the app, use **Restore** on the Subscription screen, then contact support from [Contact Us](#contact-us) if it still looks wrong.

#### About This App

**About This App** is a short product overview plus legal and support links.

- App introduction and key features
- Version and build number
- [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy)
- Third-party licenses
- Rate the app, or send feedback to [support@occudule.com](mailto:support@occudule.com)



#### Settings

**Settings** controls language, sync, alerts, time zone, and account deletion.

- **Language** — Occudule currently supports English, French, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese
- **Sync status** — whether email and calendar syncing is active, pending, or failed
- **Event conflict check range** — how far ahead Occudule looks for overlaps (default 30 days, maximum 60 days; conflict detection is a Premium or Diamond plan feature)
- **Upcoming event notifications** — optional Event Alert 1 and Event Alert 2, from a few minutes up to a week before an event
- **Time zone** — follows your phone by default; you can override it
- **Delete Account** — permanently removes your profile, children, and synced data after a confirmation. This is separate from [Log out](#log-out).



#### Contact Us

**Contact Us** opens in-app support.

- **Live chat** with Occudule support
- **Send feedback** by email to [support@occudule.com](mailto:support@occudule.com)

You can also use the website [Contact](/contact) page. Do not send payment card numbers or passwords in chat.

#### Log out

**Log out** signs you out of Occudule on this device. It does not delete your account or family data. To delete the account, use **Delete Account** in [Settings](#settings).

## Home screen

The Home screen (also the [Home tab](#home-tab)) is the first view after setup. It has two sections that both show **today's date** in the heading. Content for a new day starts at midnight.

### Today's Events

**Today's Events** lists activities scheduled for today, earliest to latest.

Each card can include the event name, institution, time, location (when Occudule has one), a short email summary, related to-dos, a link back to the original email, whether a reply is required, and any action link (permission form, school portal, or similar).

A progress bar under the section title shows completed to-dos versus total to-dos for today (red 0–30%, yellow 31–70%, green 71–100%). Each event card has a smaller bar for that event's to-dos. Tap **Show More** for full details, including school/sibling and external calendar conflicts on Premium and Diamond plan.

If nothing is on the calendar for today, Home shows a short empty message.

### Email Received Today

**Email Received Today** lists school and activity emails Occudule processed today, newest first. Receipt time is used for sorting and is not shown on the card.

Cards use the same detail pattern as Today's Events (name, institution, sender, date, summary, to-dos, reply and action flags). Pull down to refresh and sync with your mailbox. Tap **Show More** for the full record.

If you are caught up, Occudule shows **You're all caught up!** with shortcuts to add a manual event or open Settings.

Microsoft inboxes sync automatically after you connect them. Gmail users forward the messages they want processed. Both paths are in [how to process school emails in Occudule](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-process-school-emails-in-occudule).

## Footer

The footer is the bar along the bottom of every main screen. Five tabs switch the main view. The [Header](#header) stays visible so you can still filter by child or open Profile.

### Home tab

The **Home** tab opens the [Home screen](#home-screen): [Today's Events](#todays-events) and [Email Received Today](#email-received-today). Use it as the daily snapshot of what is happening and what arrived today.

### Event (Calendar) tab

The **Event** (Calendar) tab is a **7-day weekly view** that starts on today. Today's date is highlighted. Events under each day are listed earliest to latest. Days with no events show **No events**.

- Swipe left or right to change weeks, or use the week and month arrows.
- Tap **Today** to jump back to the current week.
- Tap the calendar icon for a monthly picker, then tap a date to open that week.
- A small red dot on a date means at least one event that day has a time conflict (another Occudule event and/or your connected calendar). Open the event for the two-line breakdown. Conflict detection and calendar sync are Premium and Diamond.

Each event card matches Home: name, institution, time, location, summary, to-dos, email link, reply and action flags, and **Show More**. You can also add an event by hand when something never arrived by email.

### Info tab

The **Info** tab holds **reference material** you saved from emails Occudule classified as **Info** — policies, supply lists, and other details that are not a dated event.

When a message is informational rather than a scheduled activity, confirmation asks you to treat it as **Info**. Choosing **Yes, process** saves it here instead of on the calendar. The Event versus Info choice is explained in [how to process school emails in Occudule](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-process-school-emails-in-occudule).

Use the child picker to show info for one child or for **All**.

### To-dos tab

The **To-dos** tab is a year → month → day view of tasks Occudule extracted from email or that you added yourself.

- Tap the **year** (top left) for a 12-month grid, then a month, then a day.
- Each to-do shows a status control and a deadline. An empty circle means not done; tap it to complete (solid dot). Tap the **title** to open event details and tick to-dos there too.
- A to-do deadline can differ from the parent event time. Changing the deadline does not move the event.
- Tap **+** to add a to-do for the selected day (attach it to an existing event, or add a new event first).
- Tap the magnifying glass for search across to-dos and events.

When someone in your [Family Group](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-invite-others-to-family-group) assigns you a to-do, you also get a notification. **See details** on that notification opens this tab with the item highlighted.

### Notification center tab

The **Notification center** tab is the in-app inbox for confirmations and updates.

Notifications are grouped by date (newest first). Unread rows are bold. Each card shows the time. If you chose **No, discard** on a confirmation, the card can show a **Discarded** label.

Typical types include:

- **Event confirmation** and **Info confirmation** — review a detected school email, confirm Event or Info, then **Yes, process** or **No, discard**
- **New event** / **New info** — something was saved
- **Family member joined** and **To-do assigned** — household activity

Tap an unread item to open the confirmation or detail screen. The same confirmations open from a phone push notification. Long-press to select one or more rows and mark them read or unread, or delete them. Tap the magnifying glass (or the pinned search bar after you scroll) to search notifications.

If a Gmail forward is missing, open this tab and tap **refresh**. The full confirm-and-save flow is in [how to process school emails in Occudule](/documentation/how-tos/how-to-process-school-emails-in-occudule).
