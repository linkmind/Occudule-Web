The Occudule App is a comprehensive tool designed specifically to facilitate family management in educational contexts. Here’s an in-depth look at its features:

## Email Filtering and AI Analysis

Occudule's advanced AI technology automatically scans incoming school emails and extracts critical information. This feature saves parents time by highlighting the essentials such as the child involved, school, event details, and whether a reply is needed.

## Email Processing (Gmail and Outlook)

Connect your Gmail or Outlook accounts with ease. The integration allows Occudule to process emails automatically, extracting pertinent details and ensuring that nothing important slips through the cracks.

## Attachment Analysis

Not all valuable information is contained within the body of an email. Occudule reads attachments too, pulling out events and to-do details to ensure a comprehensive overview.

## Calendar Integration

Available for Premium Plus subscribers, this feature syncs events and activities directly with your Google or Outlook Calendar, ensuring your family stays coordinated and on schedule without needing manual updates.

## Conflict Detection

Also available for Premium Plus, conflict detection keeps track of multiple schedules, alerting parents to potential clashes among siblings or other activities within their primary calendars.

## Child and School Accounts Management

Whether you have one child or several, Occudule supports the management of multiple child accounts. Each child can have designated school accounts, simplifying the integration of various educational informatics.

## Notifications

Stay informed with push notifications that provide timely reminders and alert you to action-required items. With this feature, you’re always in the loop regarding your child’s schooling needs.

## AI-powered To-Dos and Actions

Occudule transforms extracted email information into actionable tasks, providing clarity and organization for busy parents who need every day to run smoothly.

## Multi-language Support

Aiming for inclusivity, Occudule offers its interface in several languages including English, French, and Spanish, broadening its accessibility.

The Occudule App is here to help parents streamline their interactions with educational information, providing peace of mind and efficiency.
